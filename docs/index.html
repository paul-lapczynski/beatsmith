Get going

What it does

  • Three charts per song. Easy, Medium and Hard, each aimed at the difficulty number you pick.
  • Steps on the music. Arrows land on the beats and hits you hear, with holds on long notes.
  • Follows the melody. Optionally separates the vocals, so the steps can follow the singing.
  • Feel sliders. Groove, jumps, speed, breathing room, footwork, holds and repetition.
  • AI assist. A trained model can help decide where each step belongs.
  • Song artwork. Banner, background and jacket from the song's cover art.
  • Ready to play. Point the output at a StepMania Songs folder and the songs show up.
  • Chart editor. Play the song, watch the steps scroll and fix anything by hand.
StepMania, ITGmania and OutFox are their own projects; BeatSmith only writes chart files for them.
