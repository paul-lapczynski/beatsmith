Meet the app

What it does

  • Three charts per song. Easy, Medium and Hard, each aimed at the difficulty number you pick.
  • Steps on the music. Arrows land on the beats and hits you hear, with holds on long notes.
  • Experimental melody analysis. Optional vocal separation can bias steps toward the singing; it is not exact transcription.
  • Feel sliders. Groove, jumps, speed, breathing room, footwork, holds and repetition.
  • AI assist. A trained model can help decide where each step belongs.
  • Song artwork. Banner, background and jacket from the song's cover art.
  • Ready to play. Point the output at a StepMania Songs folder and the songs show up.
  • Chart editor. Play the song, watch the steps scroll and fix anything by hand.

AI, explained

Normal desktop generation processes audio on your computer. Optional local AI helps with step placement, vocal separation and verse detection. Downloads and optional artwork searches use the internet; a separate unfinished command-line review tool can contact cloud AI when explicitly run.

Claude and OpenAI Codex have also assisted development, testing and documentation. Read the full AI Usage disclosure.

Generated charts and automated scores need listening, editing and play testing. BeatSmith provides no song library.

StepMania, ITGmania and OutFox are their own projects; BeatSmith only writes chart files for them.
